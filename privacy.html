Privacy Policy — Prossible

Last updated: 2026-08-27

Prossible does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data about you.

What Prossible does

Prossible is a stage display app for live audio production. It connects to Pro Tools (a third-party audio workstation) on the same Mac or over your local network, using standard protocols (PTSL over gRPC, MIDI Time Code) to read session playback position and marker information for display purposes only. This connection is entirely local — no data is sent to us or to any external server.

Your session data

Session data (song names, markers, layout designs) is either generated by the app's built-in demo mode or read live from Pro Tools; it stays on your device or local network and is never transmitted elsewhere.

LAN View

Prossible can also act as a local network server ("LAN View"), letting band members' phones and tablets on the same network view the stage display in a browser. This data (song titles, bar counts, playback position) is streamed for local network display purposes only — it is never sent externally, never stored, and never leaves your network. Access is protected by a PIN shown only on the operator's screen.

No tracking

No account, sign-up, analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies are used.

Deletion

If you delete the app, all locally stored data (custom layouts, preferences) is removed with it.
